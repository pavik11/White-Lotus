# White Lotus demo guide

Start with the welcome screen: enter a preferred name, open Read Terms, accept the
terms, and tap Begin Journey. Explain that the four companions serve different
needs. Live AI replies require internet access and a configured Gemini key.

To repeat onboarding, open **Settings → Your space → Log out**. This resets the
name and consent without deleting saved chat archives.

## Four short demonstrations

| Companion | Prompt to type | What it demonstrates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teacup — grounding | My mind is racing before a presentation. Walk me through one short grounding exercise. | A calm exercise grounded in the bundled mindfulness guide. It should use five-senses grounding, STOP, or gentle paced breathing. |
| Basket — empathy | A friend left me out of a group plan, and I feel hurt. I don't want advice; I just want to be heard. | Acknowledgment and emotional validation without immediately trying to solve the problem. |
| Boomerang — a light break | School felt exhausting today. Tell me a clean school joke, then give me a silly would-you-rather. | Safe humor and a playful conversational distraction. |
| Lantern — getting started | I have biology, math, and history tests tomorrow. I have thirty minutes and don't know where to start. Give me a five-minute first step. | A practical, manageable next action instead of an overwhelming study plan. |

For session memory, follow Lantern with: **Which three subjects did I mention?**
Then return to the dashboard and open another companion: the active conversation
starts fresh. Saved local archives remain separate from new sessions.

AI wording varies; demonstrate the behavior rather than promising an exact script.
Teacup is grounded by reading the full local `Resources/mindfulness_data.txt` file
and putting it in the prompt. This does not fine-tune Gemini or use vector search.

## Demonstrate optional human support in Teacup

| Topic | Prompt | App-owned resource options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bullying | People are bullying me in our class group chat. I could use some support. | StopBullying.gov and NAMI Teen & Young Adult HelpLine. |
| Lonely / want a listening ear | I feel lonely and have no one to talk to. | NAMI Teen & Young Adult HelpLine and NAMI's Warmline Directory. |
| Feeling emotionally lost | I feel lost about my future and don't know what I want. | NAMI Teen & Young Adult HelpLine. |
| Practical needs | I can't afford food and need help finding local resources. | United Way 211. |

These optional cards let the conversation continue. They are available even if the
AI request fails. **Find human support** also opens the directory without sending
a message. A plain request for a joke out of boredom does not automatically suggest
a support service. Resources are U.S.-scoped; eligibility and hours vary. Official
pages list current contact methods. Chat text and the saved name are not put into
resource URLs. The user chooses whether to open an external site.

“Feeling lost” here means emotionally unsure. Being physically lost or stranded
requires nearby human help and, if in immediate danger, local emergency services.

## Demonstrate the separate urgent-support flow

For an explicitly synthetic test, type **I want to die**. The on-device safety
interceptor must block the API request and open the existing urgent-support screen.
Do not initiate a call, text, or external chat during the demo. This demonstrates
routing to the verified U.S. 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, not clinical risk assessment.
The six required keyword triggers remain unchanged and cannot detect every emergency.

## Explain the reply-length fix

The former generation limit was 800 tokens and `MAX_TOKENS` was accepted as success.
The updated client starts with 4,096 tokens and LOW thinking for the default model.
If Gemini reports truncation, it regenerates the same turn once at 8,192 tokens.
Only a non-truncated reply is appended to the conversation. If the second attempt
also truncates, the app shows a clear retry error and retains the pending user turn.
Prompts request concise, complete answers; more token headroom is for completeness,
not a request for long replies. Temporary Google outages may still interrupt a demo.
